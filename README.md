# tumor-immune-dynamics-model
MATLAB implementation and analysis of nonlinear tumor–immune dynamics under pulsed adoptive cellular immunotherapy and IL-2 treatment, based on Yang et al. (2015).

# Computational Modeling of Tumor–Immune Dynamics

### BME 3100 — University of Connecticut | Fall 2026

## Overview

This project explores mathematical modeling of tumor–immune interactions and the effects of cancer immunotherapy.

Based on the research by Yang, Tang, and Cheke (2015), the project investigates how tumor cells, immune effector cells, and interleukin-2 (IL-2) interact over time.

The goal is to use MATLAB simulations to study how immunotherapy treatment strategies may influence tumor growth and immune responses.

## Research Objectives

- Model interactions between tumor cells, immune effector cells, and IL-2.
- Implement coupled nonlinear ordinary differential equations in MATLAB.
- Investigate pulsed adoptive cellular immunotherapy (ACI).
- Examine the effects of IL-2 treatment.
- Compare simulated tumor dynamics under different treatment conditions.

## Mathematical Modeling

The model uses three interacting biological variables:

- Tumor cell population
- Immune effector cell population
- Interleukin-2 (IL-2) concentration

The mathematical framework consists of coupled nonlinear ordinary differential equations describing changes in these variables over time.

Treatment effects are investigated using numerical simulations.
